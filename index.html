About Christina

Professional training, personal coaching.

I’m Christina Katsaros, a professional Latin dancer, competitor and dance teacher offering private Ballroom and Latin lessons in Woodley.

I have spent many years training and competing at a high level in Latin American dancing, including competing internationally and becoming a 3x Portuguese National Champion.

Alongside my own competitive career, teaching has always been a huge part of what I love about dancing. I have experience teaching both children and adults, from complete beginners taking their very first lesson through to dancers working towards competitions.

My lessons are tailored to the individual. Whether you want to start dancing for fun, build confidence, improve your technique or take your competitive dancing further, I focus on helping every student become a stronger, more confident and more expressive dancer.
