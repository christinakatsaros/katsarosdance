About Christina

International experience. Personal coaching.

I'm Christina Katsaros, a professional Latin dancer, competitor and teacher offering private Ballroom and Latin dance lessons in Woodley, Reading.

I represented Portugal professionally for three years, becoming Portuguese National Professional Latin Champion three consecutive times, as well as a WDC European Professional Latin Semi-Finalist.

Throughout my competitive career, I have competed internationally at some of the most prestigious competitions in the dance world, including the World Championships in Japan, Blackpool Dance Festival, UK Open Championships, International Championships and the Dutch Open in Assen, alongside numerous other international competitions.

I have trained extensively with some of the world's leading coaches, and I bring the knowledge, technique and experience gained throughout my professional career into my teaching.

Alongside competing, teaching has become one of the parts of dancing I enjoy most. I work with children and adults at all stages, from complete beginners taking their first steps to experienced dancers preparing for competition.

My approach is personal to every student. Whether you want to dance for enjoyment, build confidence, improve your technique or develop your competitive dancing, my aim is to help you become a more knowledgeable, confident and expressive dancer.
