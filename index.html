About Christina

International experience. Personal coaching.

Christina Katsaros is a professional Latin dancer, international competitor and teacher with extensive experience on the international dance circuit.

She is a three-time Portuguese National Professional Latin Champion and WDC European Professional Latin Semi-Finalist, and has represented Portugal internationally at major events around the world.
