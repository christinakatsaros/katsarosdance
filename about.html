About Christina

A lifetime in dance.

Professional Latin dancer, international competitor and teacher Christina Katsaros.

The Story

From first competition to the world stage.

Dance has been part of Christina's life from childhood.

What began with her first competitions developed into a professional international career in Latin American dancing.

Christina represented Portugal professionally for three years, becoming Portuguese National Professional Latin Champion three consecutive times.

She went on to become a WDC European Professional Latin Semi-Finalist and competed at the WDC World Professional Dance Championships in Japan.

International Career

Competing at the highest level.

Throughout her professional competitive career, Christina competed at many of the most prestigious Ballroom and Latin competitions in the world.

Her competitive experience includes the Blackpool Dance Festival, UK Open Championships, International Championships, Dutch Open in Assen and the WDC World Professional Dance Championships in Japan, alongside numerous other international events.

During her career, Christina trained extensively with some of the world's leading coaches, developing the technical, performance and artistic knowledge that now forms the foundation of her teaching.

From Competitor to Teacher

Passing the knowledge forward.

Christina's aim is not simply to teach steps, but to help students understand their movement and become confident, expressive dancers.

Every dancer learns differently. Lessons are therefore adapted to the individual rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach.

Whether a student is entering the studio for the first time or preparing for competition, the same care is given to building strong foundations, technical understanding and genuine enjoyment of dance.
