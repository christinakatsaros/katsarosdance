About Christina

A lifetime in dance.

Professional Latin dancer, international competitor and teacher Christina Katsaros.

The Story

From first competition to the world stage.

Dance has been part of Christina's life from childhood.

What began with her first competitions developed into a professional international career in Latin American dancing, with competition, training and performance becoming a major part of her life.

Christina represented Portugal professionally for three years and became Portuguese National Professional Latin Champion three consecutive times.

She is also a WDC European Professional Latin Semi-Finalist and has competed at the WDC World Professional Dance Championships in Japan.

Christina continues to bring her professional dance experience into both her own development and her teaching, combining high-level technical knowledge with an individual and supportive approach to every student.

International Experience

Competing at the highest level.

Christina has competed at many of the most prestigious Ballroom and Latin competitions in the world.

Her international experience includes the Blackpool Dance Festival, UK Open Championships, International Championships, Dutch Open in Assen and the WDC World Professional Dance Championships in Japan, alongside numerous other events.

Throughout her training and competitive experience, Christina has worked extensively with some of the world's leading coaches, developing the technical, performance and artistic knowledge that forms the foundation of her dancing and teaching today.

Professional Dancer & Teacher

Experience from the floor. Individual coaching in the studio.

Christina brings the same attention to detail, discipline and understanding of movement from her professional dancing into every lesson she teaches.

Her aim is not simply to teach steps, but to help students understand their movement and become confident, expressive dancers.

Every dancer learns differently. Lessons are therefore adapted to the individual rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach.

Whether a student is entering the studio for the first time or preparing for competition, the focus is on strong foundations, technical understanding, musicality and genuine enjoyment of dance.
